Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-26 · Pre-launch

Waystone AI ("we", "us") is a niche directory for AI developer tools, operated by an independent developer. This policy explains what we collect on this pre-launch site and how we use it. We keep data collection to the minimum needed to run the reservation and, later, the directory.

What we collect

  • Reservation form: tool name, product page URL, one-line description, category, email address, chosen tier, and your consent selections.
  • Technical data: standard server logs (IP address, browser type, timestamps) collected by our hosting provider for security and operations.
  • Later, at launch: if you sign in with Google, we receive your Google account identifier, email, name, and avatar to create your account. We do not request access to your Google contacts, files, or other sensitive data.

How we use it

  • To contact you about your reservation and notify you when listings open.
  • To review and publish your tool listing.
  • To operate, secure, and improve the site.

We do not sell your personal data, and we do not use it for advertising.

Third parties

  • Form processor (e.g. Formspree / Tally): handles reservation form submissions on our behalf.
  • Hosting provider (e.g. Cloudflare / Vercel): serves the site and stores server logs.
  • Google: authentication at launch, under Google's OAuth terms.
  • Payment processor: a merchant-of-record platform will handle payments at launch; we do not store card details.

Retention & your rights

We keep reservation data only as long as needed to run the directory and meet legal obligations. You may request access, correction, or deletion of your data at any time by emailing hello@waystoneai.online. We will respond within a reasonable period.

Cookies

This pre-launch page uses no tracking cookies. At launch, any essential session cookies used for sign-in will be described here.
